Twelve tiny browser toys, in four families. Each turns one gesture into a small workout for a specific cognitive skill, from attention to prediction to mental rotation. Made by aaryaa as a play-based-learning study. The label under each card names the skill it exercises. Pick one and press, drag, or let go.

Twelve gestures, twelve skills.

Hold Press and hold. Attention kept in one place, over time.
Draw Drag a line. A gesture you can see and hear at once.
Let go Aim, release, and read what your rules produce.
Look Track it, uncover it, turn it to fit. Ways of seeing closely.

A study in embodied inquiry: exercising a cognitive skill by doing it, not reading about it. One self-contained page, no backend, works offline once loaded. Sound is on in several toys. Best with the volume up.
